# Defense & Intelligence Applications

## Overview

The Morphic Bitstream Engine (MBE) has significant potential applications in defense and intelligence sectors due to its unique capabilities:

- **Processes unknown data** without prior knowledge of formats
- **Detects anomalies** by identifying structural changes in data streams
- **Hardware-level speed** faster than software-based analysis
- **Adversarial resistant** harder to evade than signature-based systems
- **Self-adapting** no manual reconfiguration needed
